
**About This Dashboard**

**Purpose**

The Enterprise Executive Overview brings together system authorization, findings, remediation plans and exception requests across business units. It helps leaders identify areas needing attention, prioritize follow-up and review progress using a common reporting view.

**What the dashboard shows**

| Area | Business meaning |
|---|---|
| **System Authorization Status** | Authorization and POA&M information from Archer A&A, alongside SAP risk scores from ACP. |
| **Site Assessment & Audit Status** | Findings, remediation plans and exception requests from Archer Issue Management. |

**Understanding the measures**

| Measure | What it means and why it matters |
|---|---|
| **ATO: Authorization to Operate** | A formal decision authorizing a system to operate with accepted risk. Authorization status helps identify packages needing renewal or follow-up. A current ATO does not mean there are no security issues. |
| **POA&M: Plan of Action and Milestones** | A plan documenting corrective actions and milestones for identified weaknesses. Status shows the recorded progress of that work. |
| **Average SAP Risk Score** | Summarizes SAP risk scores by business using the ACP scoring method. Interpret it using the ACP score scale, alongside authorization and findings information. |
| **Open Findings** | Findings currently recorded as Open. Remediation due windows help identify overdue work and upcoming deadlines. |
| **Open Findings by Policy Level 3** | Groups open findings by their directly assigned Level 3 policies, helping identify policy areas requiring attention. |
| **Remediation Plans** | Plans for addressing identified issues. Status and estimated completion windows help track delivery and follow-up. |
| **Exception Requests** | Requests to depart from a requirement. Risk and review dates help identify requests needing attention. A request does not, by itself, indicate approval. |

**How to use the dashboard**

- Select the business unit you want to review.
- Review status, risk and overdue items together to understand priorities.
- Open **Findings Details** or **Remediation Details** to review individual records.
- Check active filters when comparing results between pages.

**How to read the numbers**

- **Policy counts can overlap.** One finding can relate to several policies. Each policy counts its linked findings, so adding the policy bars can exceed the overall open-findings total.
- **Missing policy assignments are excluded from named policy categories.** Those findings remain part of the overall open-findings population.
- **Shared business memberships can overlap.** Use the overall total rather than adding category totals where an item belongs to multiple groups.
- **No Date means the relevant date is missing.** It does not mean the item is on time.
- **Assigned To shows available employee names.** Missing names do not necessarily mean that a finding is unassigned.
- **Results reflect available source data.** Report refresh time and source update time can differ.
